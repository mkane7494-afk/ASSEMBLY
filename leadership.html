The story

From the floor to the platform.

Michael Kane founded Assembly Business Systems on a simple conviction: the businesses that build things deserve the same calibre of systems as the enterprises they compete with. As Managing Director, he sets the direction for every engagement, every product and the standard Assembly holds itself to.

His edge is that he has done the work. Fifteen years across the automotive trade, manufacturing, and sales and marketing gave him a working knowledge of how a business really runs: where quotes stall, where stock disappears, where margin quietly leaks, and where good people lose hours to bad software.

Then he went further than most operators ever do. Michael led systems implementations from the inside, then started designing and building the software himself, including BuildTrack Pro, Assembly’s own platform for vehicle builders, fabricators and manufacturers.

That combination is rare, and it is the foundation Assembly is built on. The person who maps your process understands the trade, speaks the language of the floor and the boardroom, and is accountable for the system that ships.
