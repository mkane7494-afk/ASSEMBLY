Privacy policy

Your information, handled properly.

Last updated 13 November 2026

Who we are

Assembly Business Systems Co Pty Ltd (ABN 69 703 626 831) is a technology company based on the Gold Coast, Queensland. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through this website and in the course of our business, in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry through this website or contact us directly, we collect the details you choose to give us — typically your name, company, email address, phone number and the content of your message. We may also collect basic, aggregated information about how the website is used (for example, pages viewed and the type of device), which does not identify you.

Why we collect it

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, to provide and improve our services, and to keep in touch about work you have asked us about. We do not sell your personal information.

How we store and protect it

Enquiries are held in our email and customer management systems, including BuildTrack Pro. We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers who help us run our business — such as website hosting, email and software providers — and only as needed for those services. Some of these providers may store data outside Australia. We do not otherwise disclose your information unless required or authorised by law.

Accessing or correcting your information

You can ask to access or correct the personal information we hold about you at any time by emailing info@assemblysystems.com.au.

Questions and complaints

If you have a question or concern about how we have handled your personal information, please contact us at info@assemblysystems.com.au or 0439 308 942 and we will respond within a reasonable time. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available on this page.
